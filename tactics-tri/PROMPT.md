   - Pincer/follow-up: when an attacker hits a target and an ally stands on the exact opposite side of the target, the ally performs a free follow-up attack.
   - TP: each unit has TP pool (max 5ish), +1 at start of its turn; skills cost TP.
   - Terrain: e.g. grass normal, forest +evasion, water slows, shallow ruins/high walls etc.
   - Vote ("信念天平"): party members each have conviction leanings; each casts a vote between two choices; player may persuade 1-2 members (simple persuasion check based on dialogue choice); majority decides; tie-break rule defined. Result selects next battle.
