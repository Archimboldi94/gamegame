# Audio credits

All files currently under `audio/sfx/` and `audio/bgm/` are **procedurally generated placeholders** produced by `tools/gen_sfx.py` (simple synthesized tones/noise, encoded to Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz).

They are original to this project, not sampled from commercial libraries, and exist only as stand-ins until real SFX/BGM are authored or licensed. See `audio/README.md` for the swap-in file names.
